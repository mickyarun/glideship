## What the AI does and doesn't do

When AI drafting is on, it writes a few lines about your month from the work Glideship recorded: cards, bugs, BUDs, pull requests and reviews. Every line shows the records it came from, so you can check it.

- It never scores you, compares you with anyone, or judges your work.
- It only sees your records, the headings for your role and what you wrote yourself.
- Your manager scores, HR checks the spread, and people decide. The AI never does.

When AI drafting is off, you see the same records as a plain list and write your own lines. Nothing on the page is written by AI.

## Who sees what

- Your check-ins: you, your managers and HR.
- Your manager's feedback: you, as soon as they save it.
- Your self-score: you, then your manager and HR once you submit it.
- Your manager's score and reasons: you, once HR shares the result.
- Answers only your manager gives, such as readiness for the next level: never you.
