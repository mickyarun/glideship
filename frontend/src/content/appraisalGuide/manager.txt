## Giving feedback

Write about the work, not the person. One line to keep doing, one to try next.

- Thin: "Good attitude."
- Good: "Keep: catching regressions before UAT. Try: pair on the refunds UAT next month."

Your report sees what you save straight away. Feedback you never write stays "not done" on the team list and in the fairness check. It never blocks the review.

## Agreeing or changing a rating

Agree when the evidence supports their rating. Change it when it doesn't, and write a reason they will read: name the records or goals that moved you. Changing to either "Exceeded" rating needs two examples, and to "Did not meet" one.

## Due dates and reminders

Each piece of work has a due date. You get one Slack message per reminder covering all your reports, with counts and dates only, never names or notes. Reminders stop as soon as everything is done.
