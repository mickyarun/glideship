## Setting up a cycle

Create the cycle from the org settings, then check readiness before you open it: people without a primary manager aren't enrolled, people without a matching role template get the general one, and possible duplicate accounts should be merged first so their work shows.

## Role templates

Each template says what the four headings mean for a role, which records count and which goals it suggests. People are matched by department or designation; you can override the match for a cycle.

## Reading calibration

Look at the spread of bands for each manager, how often they agreed with their reports, and the large gaps. Send a review back with a note when it needs another look, then share results.
