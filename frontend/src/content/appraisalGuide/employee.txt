## Writing a good check-in line

Say what happened and what came of it. Short and specific beats long and vague.

- Thin: "Doing my best on testing."
- Good: "Ran the daily suite; caught 3 regressions before UAT."
- Thin: "Helped the team."
- Good: "Paired with Sam on the refunds API until it passed review."

Add what Glideship missed: a link, a win nobody filed, a blocker. If a heading is empty, say so or leave it; an empty month is shown as missed and never held against you.

## Picking goals

Up to three a period. Each one says what done looks like and where the number comes from, for example "UAT automation at 80%, from the test report".

## Scoring yourself

Pick the band that fits and say why. Bands 1 and 2 need at least two pieces of evidence: records from your check-ins, links or goal values.
